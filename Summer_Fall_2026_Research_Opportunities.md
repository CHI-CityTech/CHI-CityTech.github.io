# Summer/Fall 2026 Research Opportunities

The Center for Holistic Integration (CHI) invites students, faculty, and collaborators to join active research pathways for Summer and Fall 2026.

## Priority Focus: OAA Self-Aware Room (SAR)

A major active initiative for this cycle is the OAA-supported Self-Aware Room (SAR) development effort within the Blended Reality Performance System (BRPS) ecosystem.

Current opportunity areas include:

- immersive environment design and systems integration
- sensing, mediation, and responsive interaction workflows
- spatial audio and real-time virtual environment integration
- ethical and collaborative artificial intelligence (AI) integration
- documentation, operations, and research communication

## Additional Opportunity Areas

In addition to SAR, CHI continues active opportunities across:

- Balanced Blended Space (BBS) theory and syntax
- Blended Shadow Puppet (BSP) development
- curriculum and open educational resource development
- media, design, and public-facing communications
- interdisciplinary technical implementation and prototyping
